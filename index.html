About us

A family company since 1987

Gary Bailey started the company in Orange Park in October 1987. Nearly four decades later it is still owned and run by the Bailey family, and the owners stay involved in every project from bid to closeout.

Gary and his son Jason both hold Florida certified building contractor licenses. Gary leads the company as President, and Jason runs day-to-day operations, estimating and project management. When you call the office, you talk to the people who will build your project.

How we work

On schedule, on campus, by the book

The owners are on every job. A principal manages the project and a full-time superintendent runs the site every day.

Scheduled to the critical path

Every project gets a CPM schedule broken out by trade and updated monthly with the owner and architect. Long-lead equipment is released early.

Built from approved submittals

Nothing is ordered until the architect has reviewed it. Testing and inspections are scheduled at every stage, and every punch-list item is closed.

Safe around students

Everyone on site is Jessica Lunsford Act cleared. Work areas are fenced off, and noisy work is coordinated with school administration.

Clean closeout

As-builts, O&M manuals, manufacturer warranties and our own one-year warranty, turned over at the end of the job.
